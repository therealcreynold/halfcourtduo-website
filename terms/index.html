What Half Court Duo sells

The game is free to download and free to play, every mode included. It sells two kinds of thing, through Apple, and the price of each is the one the App Store shows you before you buy:

  • Remove Ads, bought once, which removes every advert, the optional videos included. The "+30 s" overtime and the free power-ups the videos paid for stay, with no video to watch. It belongs to your Apple Account and comes back with Restore Purchases in the game's Settings, on any device signed in to that account, through Family Sharing, and after any reinstall.
  • Power-up packs: a stated number of uses of Slow-Mo, Steady Hoop or Hot Start, or five of each in the Power-Up Bundle, for Arcade rounds only. They are consumable: each use is spent when a round starts with it switched on, they never expire, and they are kept on the device they were bought on. Restore Purchases cannot bring them back, and deleting the game deletes any you have left. Support says exactly what each one does.

There are no coins, no currency, no loot boxes, no subscriptions and nothing else for sale, and nothing you buy is random. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, not by us.

If anything else is ever added for sale, its price and what it gives will be set out here and in the game before anybody can buy it.
