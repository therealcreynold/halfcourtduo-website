Hinge Hoops

Fold it. Flick it. Swish.

An arcade basketball machine that lives in your folded phone. Stand it up like a laptop: the hoop is on the raised half, you flick from the half on the table, and every shot sails up and over the fold.

Made for a phone that folds

Coming to the App Store. Free, with ads.

Plays on any iPhone with iOS 27.1 or later. Best folded like a laptop.

Sixty seconds on the clock

Flick, swish, flick again.

The next ball racks under half a second after the last one leaves your thumb, and up to three can be in the air at once. Flick up from the ball on the pad; a tap does nothing.

  • 2

    Two points a basket.

  • +1

    Nothing but net is worth one more. A basket that never touches the rim is a swish.

  • 3

    The last fifteen seconds. The backboard turns red and every basket thrown then is worth three.

  • +1

    Heat. Make three in a row and the ball catches fire: every basket after that is worth one more, until you miss.

  • 20 40

    A hoop that moves. At 20 points it starts to slide; at 40 it moves faster and the backboard tilts.

  • 0:00

    Beat the buzzer. A shot in the air when the buzzer goes still counts.

Four ways to play

All four are on the main menu, and none of them needs an account.

Arcade

Sixty seconds, as many baskets as you can.

Streak

No clock. Miss once and it is over.

Daily Shootout

One hoop for everyone each day. The same moving pattern and the same two bonus windows for every player, and a fresh leaderboard at midnight UTC.

Pass & Play

Two players, one phone, thirty seconds each. Hand it across the table and see who wins.

Every way you hold it

Folded like a laptop is the best seat in the house, and it plays every other way as well.

  • Folded like a laptop. The hoop on the raised half, the court on the half on the table, and the ball crossing the fold on every shot.
  • Open flat. The fold becomes a brass rail across the middle, and every shot flies over it.
  • On its side, like a book. A side view of the same machine, and you throw toward the hoop across the fold. Turning the folded phone between laptop and book never pauses a round.
  • Closed. The whole game on the outside screen, with the brass rail where the fold would be.
  • A phone without a fold. The same rail across the middle of the screen, and the same game.

Open it flat in the middle of a round and the game waits for you to fold it back, or carry on flat with Resume.

A real cabinet, drawn in code

Chasing bulbs, a neon sign, LED scoreboards, a net that swishes and a ball that catches fire. Every light is drawn in code and every sound is synthesized: the clank of the rim, the horn at the buzzer, the crowd when you heat up. Feel the rim and the swish through your fingers too.

No sample packs and no downloaded art: the bulbs, the net, the ball, the lettering on the signs and every sound are made by the game's own code.

Power-ups, if you want them

Three power-ups for Arcade rounds, sold in packs. Slow-Mo runs the clock and the hoop at three quarters speed, so sixty seconds on the board last eighty. Steady Hoop keeps the hoop still all round. Hot Start sets the ball on fire at the start and keeps it lit for the first fifteen seconds, misses and all. Switch one on beside PLAY and it is used when the round starts. A powered round keeps its own best and is never posted to Game Center, and the Daily Shootout, Streak and Pass & Play are always played without them. Power-ups stay on your phone and cannot be restored.

About the ads and purchases, plainly

Hinge Hoops is free. A banner sits under the menu and the results, never during a round. A full screen ad can follow a finished round, after the buzzer and before your results: never within 45 seconds of the last one or of opening the game, and never in the middle of a Pass & Play match. At the buzzer of an Arcade or Daily Shootout round you can choose to watch a video for thirty more seconds, once a round; that round counts for your own best but not for the leaderboards. In the power-up shop you can choose to watch a video for a free power-up, up to three a day.

Remove Ads, one purchase, removes every ad, the videos included. The +30 s overtime and the free power-ups stay, with no video to watch. Apart from Remove Ads and the power-up packs nothing is for sale: no coins, no energy, no loot boxes.

What the advertising collects, and every choice you have about it, is in the privacy policy.
