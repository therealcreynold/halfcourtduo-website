Half Court Duo privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Half Court Duo is an arcade basketball game made by CR Industries LLC. This page says what the game collects, who else collects something while you play, and what you can choose.

What Half Court Duo collects

Nothing, of its own. There is no Half Court Duo account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no server of ours that the game talks to. Your settings, your best scores and records, the names you type for Pass & Play, a round you leave in the middle, and the counters that space out the adverts are all kept on your phone, and they go when you delete the game.

Apple's services

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to it, your scores and your progress towards the achievements go to Apple, not to us, so they can appear on the leaderboards and in Game Center. Pass & Play matches are never posted, and a round extended with the "+30 s" video never reaches a leaderboard. Apple's privacy policy governs Game Center, and you can manage or remove that data in Apple's Game Center settings.

Purchases are handled by Apple. When you buy Remove Ads or restore it, Apple takes the payment; the game only asks Apple, on your phone, whether Remove Ads is yours. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

Advertising is what keeps the game free, and it comes from one company: Google, through AdMob. Google's software in the game collects:

  • A device identifier, used to count how often an advert has been shown, to measure adverts and to detect fraud. Apple's advertising identifier is shared only if you allow tracking (see below).
  • Approximate location, worked out from your internet address, at about the level of a city or region. Never your precise location.
  • How you interact with adverts: that one was shown, and whether it was tapped or watched.
  • Advertising data, such as which adverts you have seen.
  • Diagnostics, such as a record of a crash or of how fast an advert loaded.

Where the adverts appear: a banner under the main menu and the results, never during a round; a full screen advert after some finished rounds (at most one every third round, at least 90 seconds apart, and none in your first session); and an optional video you can choose to watch at the buzzer for thirty more seconds. Nothing in the game requires you to watch anything.

Google's privacy policy governs what Google does with this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What you can choose

Adverts chosen for you. After your first finished round, the results screen explains that the game is paid for by adverts, and then iOS asks whether Half Court Duo may track you. If you allow it, Google may use Apple's advertising identifier to choose adverts for you. If you do not, you still get the whole game and every reward, and the adverts are not chosen using that identifier. You can change your answer at any time in your phone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

The Personalised ads switch, in the game's Settings under Ads, turns adverts chosen for you off, from then on, whatever your other answers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any advert is requested, and Settings, Ads, Privacy options lets you change it. In the American states with their own privacy laws, the same Privacy options row is where you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Saying no costs you nothing: the game is the same either way.

Remove Ads is one purchase that removes the banner and the full screen adverts between rounds. It does not remove the optional "+30 s" videos, which stay available, and are always your choice; so Google's software still starts, and your choices above still apply, after you buy it.

Report an ad. If you see an advert that should not be there, Settings, Ads, Report an ad tells you how to send it to us, and we can block that advertiser for everyone playing.

Sharing a score

The Share button on the results screen makes a picture of your score on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it, with the names you typed if it was a Pass & Play match.

Children

Half Court Duo is not directed at children under 13, is not in the Kids category, and does not knowingly collect information from children. The adverts are limited to Google's general audiences rating.

Keeping and deleting

We keep nothing, because nothing reaches us. Everything the game keeps is on your phone and is removed when you delete the game. Your Game Center scores and achievements belong to your Game Center account with Apple, so they stay after the game is deleted. What Google keeps is governed by Google's policies.

Changes

If what the game collects changes, this page changes before the update that changes it ships, with a new date at the top.
